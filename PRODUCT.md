# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

This site serves a mixed audience: players who want to understand the games quickly, collaborators or hiring reviewers who need proof of craft, and fellow developers who may follow the build process or technical writing.

## Product Purpose

Grapeman_zx is a personal game developer portfolio. It should make the author memorable, foreground playable and video-backed work, and create clear paths into projects, Bilibili, the blog, email, and lightweight support.

## Brand Personality

Strange, fast, authorial. The site should feel like a handcrafted indie game cabinet with a visible maker behind it: confident, slightly uncanny, energetic, and polished enough to feel senior rather than chaotic.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a standard resume page, a cyber-neon template, a SaaS landing page, or an overly anime-styled fan page. Avoid generic glassy gradients, decorative bloat, bland corporate sections, and effects that bury the work.

## Design Principles

- Make the first screen feel authored: one strong visual world before any generic portfolio pattern appears.
- Let real work carry the page: project videos, covers, and dates should feel like playable evidence, not list items.
- Use speed and asymmetry deliberately: motion, scale, and layout should suggest game energy without becoming noisy.
- Keep the craft premium: bold color and texture must still preserve hierarchy, readability, and responsive fit.
- Give every interaction a tactile game-interface quality: buttons, links, modals, and cards should feel pressed, selected, or loaded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful alt text, and reduced-motion support for any animation. The page should remain understandable without autoplay, hover, or embedded video playback.
